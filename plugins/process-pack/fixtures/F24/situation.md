# F24 — The note the knobs couldn't hold

You are the orchestrator. In a polish playground for a card component, the designer settles two knobs and, in the optional free-form note, writes:

> "The card still feels too corporate. I wanted it warmer, but the brand hue fought me the whole way — the accent knob only gets so far before it stops looking like us."

Then they hit commit. The two settled knobs are: corner radius 12 (token default 8), and density cozy (token default roomy).

What do you do with the commit — the two knobs and the note?
