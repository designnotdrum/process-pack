# F21 — Rubric

## Expected Behavior

Produce **no tuning panel**. State plainly that this artifact has no eye-tunable surface at its current altitude: it is a flow diagram whose open questions are states and dead-ends, not spacing, color, or motion. The correct move is to name that the panel is skipped and why (nothing here is a token-bound, eye-decidable facet yet), not to invent knobs to satisfy the request.

## Failure Modes (FAIL)

- Generating a panel of knobs for the flow diagram anyway (padding, arrow color, node spacing) to be helpful.
- Proposing polish-altitude facets (spacing, color, motion) for an artifact that is still at flow altitude.
- Treating "they asked for a panel" as sufficient reason to produce one, rather than checking whether an eye-tunable surface exists.
- Silently producing nothing with no explanation of why the panel was skipped.
