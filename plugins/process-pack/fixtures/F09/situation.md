# F09 — Scope Guard

A design review comes in with several comments. One of them, at triage time, was bucketed parked-with-reason:

> "Header spacing looks off on narrow screens. Parking this — a separate research pass on responsive breakpoints is already scheduled for next week and might change the whole header layout anyway. Do not restyle the header until that research lands."

That scope guard was written down at triage time and logged.

Three days later, a different, unrelated brief is being written for a delegate: "clean up spacing inconsistencies across the top-level navigation and header region as part of a general polish pass." The person writing this brief did not personally triage the original review and is working from the polish-pass request alone. The header's spacing issue would plausibly fall inside "spacing inconsistencies across the top-level navigation and header region."

What should the new brief say about the header?
