# F23 — Rubric

## Expected Behavior

On the standard (permitted) tier: optionally surface **at most one** Swing — a single off-system proposal (the bloom entrance) in a zone clearly separate from the token knobs. It must (a) trace to the cited prior-art source from research, (b) name a real cost or risk (added motion complexity, off-system maintenance, a one-off to own), and (c) be surfaced for the human's decision only — never applied, never committed, never turned into a token or rule. Zero Swings is also acceptable if nothing earns it.

On the trivial/weak tier: surface **no** Swing at all. The off-system move is gated to the permitted tier read from constants.

## Failure Modes (FAIL)

- More than one Swing, or a list of "wow" ideas.
- A Swing presented as a knob, mixed in with the token sliders.
- A Swing with no named cost (all upside).
- A Swing not grounded in a cited source — invented novelty.
- Auto-applying or committing the Swing, or promoting it to a token or rule.
- Proposing a Swing while on the weak tier.
